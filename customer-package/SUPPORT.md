# Customer Support & Assistance

Thank you for choosing **QuotePilot**. We are committed to ensuring you have a seamless experience creating high-converting proposals.

---

## 1. Before You Reach Out

Most common setup and formatting questions can be resolved in under two minutes:
1. **PDF Formatting / Backgrounds:** Check Section 1 of `TROUBLESHOOTING.md`. Ensure **Background graphics** is checked in your browser's print dialog.
2. **Missing Proposals / Restoring Data:** Check Section 2 of `TROUBLESHOOTING.md`. Ensure you use **Settings > Import JSON** to restore from your backup file.
3. **General Workflow Questions:** Review the step-by-step instructions in `USER-GUIDE.md` and `FAQ.md`.

---

## 2. Contacting Support

If you have encountered a bug, discovered an unexpected issue, or need technical assistance with your purchase:

- **Payhip Store Support:** Contact us directly through your **Payhip Customer Account** or via the contact form on our official Payhip store page.
- **Direct Support Email:** `support@quotepilot.studio` (or the direct seller contact email provided on your Payhip order receipt).
- **Typical Response Time:** We aim to reply to all inquiries within **24–48 business hours** (Monday through Friday).

---

## 3. How to Submit an Effective Support Request

To help us resolve your issue as quickly as possible, please include:
1. **Your Payhip Order Number / Receipt ID** (for license verification).
2. **Operating System:** (e.g., macOS Sonoma, Windows 11, Ubuntu 24.04).
3. **Web Browser & Version:** (e.g., Google Chrome v128, Safari 17.5).
4. **Description of the Issue:** What were you trying to do, and what happened instead?
5. **Screenshots or Console Logs:** If possible, attach a screenshot of the issue or any error messages shown in your browser’s developer console (`F12` > `Console`).

---

## 4. Updates & Maintenance

When an updated version of QuotePilot is released on Payhip:
1. Log into your Payhip account to download the latest product package.
2. Before updating your files, open your current QuotePilot app, go to **Settings**, and click **Export JSON Backup**.
3. Install or deploy the new version.
4. Go to **Settings** > **Import JSON**, select your backup file, and all your existing proposals, clients, and templates will carry over effortlessly.

---

## 5. Feature Requests & Feedback

QuotePilot was created by independent creators for independent creators. If you have an idea for an industry template, new milestone structure, or workflow improvement:
- Send your thoughts to `feedback@quotepilot.studio`.
