## 1. What QuotePilot Is

QuotePilot is an editorial-grade web application designed to bridge the gap between receiving an informal client project request and closing the deal with an ironclad, executive-level proposal document.

Instead of starting from blank documents, wrestling with messy spreadsheets, or paying recurring monthly subscription fees for bulky enterprise software, QuotePilot provides a streamlined, opinionated workflow. In under three minutes, you can configure project scope, itemize deliverables, structure payment milestones, define strict scope boundaries to eliminate scope-creep, and generate a beautiful, printable PDF proposal with digital sign-off.

## 3. What You Can Do With QuotePilot

With QuotePilot running on your machine or hosted on your private domain, you can:
- **Build Proposals with a 7-Step Guided Wizard:** Move step-by-step from client contact info and project timeline to line items, taxes, payment terms, and scope boundaries.
- **Launch from 6 Pre-Built Industry Templates:** Instant starting points for Web Development, UI/UX Design, Conversion Copywriting, Video Production, Growth Marketing, and Automation Workflows.
- **Sell Packaged Services:** Use the 3-tier package comparison matrix (Basic, Growth Pro, Enterprise Bespoke) to offer tiered pricing and spin up proposals in one click.
- **Eliminate Scope Creep Before It Starts:** Explicitly define "What's Included", "What's Not Included", revision limits (e.g., 2 consolidated rounds), and client responsibilities.
- **Structure Clear Payment Milestones:** Choose from standard payment schedules (50/50 Deposit & Launch, 30/70 Retainer & Delivery, 33/33/34 Phased Milestones, 100% Upfront) or configure custom percentage triggers.
- **Generate Print & PDF Documents:** One-click export to clean, print-optimized PDF with studio branding, tabular financial figures, and executive styling.
- **Enable Digital Client Acceptance:** Capture digital sign-off and approval timestamps right in the document viewer.
- **Track Your Pipeline:** Monitor total pipeline value, sent proposals, accepted deals, and closing rates on your personal dashboard.
- **Manage Your Client Directory:** Maintain a local rolodex of clients with their proposal history and total lifetime quoted value.
- **Own 100% of Your Data:** Zero monthly subscriptions. Everything is saved locally in your browser with one-click JSON backup and restore.

## 4. How Your Data Is Stored & Protected

**100% Private. Local-First. Zero Cloud Lock-In.**

- **Browser LocalStorage:** QuotePilot stores all your proposals, clients, custom templates, pricing packages, and studio profile settings directly in your browser's persistent `localStorage`.
- **Zero Third-Party Servers:** Your client names, emails, project scopes, and pricing numbers are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party database.
- **No Recurring Fees:** You purchased QuotePilot once; there are no monthly fees, paywalls, or feature tiers.
- **One-Click JSON Backups:** Export your entire workspace (proposals, clients, templates, settings) to a single portable `.json` file at any time from the **Studio Settings** tab. Restore or transfer your data across computers in seconds.

## 6. How to Get Started in Under 5 Minutes

1. **Open the Application:** Run `npm install && npm run dev` in your project folder, or open the hosted web application link provided with your purchase.
2. **Set Up Your Studio Profile:** Go to **Studio Settings** in the sidebar. Enter your name, business name, monogram logo mark (e.g. `QP`), email, phone, and default currency symbol. Click **Save Changes**.
3. **Pick a Template or Package:** Navigate to **Templates** or **Packages**, choose a service matching your project, and click **Use Template**.
4. **Customize & Polish:** Adjust client details, review the line items, confirm payment terms, and proceed to **Review & Save**.
5. **Export & Deliver:** Click **Export / Print PDF** to save a pristine PDF document or copy the proposal link for your client.

*For complete step-by-step instructions, open `QUICK-START-GUIDE.md`.*
