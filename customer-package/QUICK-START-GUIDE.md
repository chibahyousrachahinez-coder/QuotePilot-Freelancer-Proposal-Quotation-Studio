# QuotePilot Quick Start Guide

**Get up and running with your proposal studio in under 5 minutes.**

---

## Step 1: Launch the Application

### Option A: Running Locally (Node.js Environment)
If you received the full source code package:
1. Ensure you have **Node.js** (v18 or higher) installed on your computer.
2. Open your terminal in the application root directory.
3. Install dependencies:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```
4. Start the local development server:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
5. Open your browser and navigate to `http://localhost:3000` (or the URL displayed in your terminal).

### Option B: Using Hosted / Static Web Build
If you are accessing QuotePilot via a pre-deployed link or hosted static build:
- Simply open the web URL in any modern browser (Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Arc).
- QuotePilot runs directly in your browser with zero server installation required.

---

## Step 2: Configure Your Studio Profile (1 Minute)

Before sending your first proposal, make sure the document displays your business branding and contact information:

1. Click **Settings** in the left-hand sidebar.
2. Fill out the **Freelancer & Studio Profile** fields:
   - **Freelancer Full Name:** (e.g., *Alex Morgan*)
   - **Professional Title:** (e.g., *Senior Full-Stack Architect & UI Designer*)
   - **Studio / Business Name:** (e.g., *Vanguard Digital Studio*)
   - **Logo Monogram Mark:** Enter 1 to 3 letters to appear in the header seal (e.g., *VG* or *QP*).
   - **Contact Email:** Your direct client-facing email.
   - **Phone Number:** Optional phone or WhatsApp contact.
   - **Portfolio / Website:** Your website or portfolio link.
3. Review **Financial & Billing Defaults**:
   - **Currency Symbol:** Select `$`, `€`, `£`, `¥`, or `CHF`.
   - **Currency ISO Code:** Set your currency code (e.g., `USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`).
   - **Default Tax Rate (%):** Set to `0` if you don't collect VAT/sales tax, or your local rate (e.g., `10` or `20`).
4. Click **Save Changes** in the top-right corner.

---

## Step 3: Add Your First Client (30 Seconds)

You can save clients once and reuse them across multiple proposals:

1. Click **Clients** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Client** in the top right.
3. Enter:
   - **Client Name:** (e.g., *Sarah Jenkins*)
   - **Company Name:** (e.g., *Acme Health Labs*)
   - **Email Address:** (e.g., *sarah@acmehealth.io*)
   - **Phone & Website:** (Optional)
   - **Notes:** (e.g., *Referred by Dave; prefers milestone billing*)
4. Click **Save Client**.

*(Note: You can also type new client details directly on the fly when creating a proposal!)*

---

## Step 4: Create Your First Proposal in Under 3 Minutes

You have two fast ways to create a proposal:

### Method A: From a Pre-Built Template (Fastest)
1. In the sidebar, click **Templates**.
2. Select one of the 6 specialized industry templates:
   - *Web Development & Custom App*
   - *UI/UX Design System*
   - *Conversion Copywriting*
   - *Video & Motion Production*
   - *Strategic Growth Marketing*
   - *Automation & Workflow Systems*
3. Click **Use Template**.
4. QuotePilot will automatically load the full 7-step wizard pre-populated with realistic line items, timelines, payment terms, and scope boundaries.
5. In **Step 1 (Client)**, pick the client you created in Step 3.
6. Click **Next** through the steps to adjust pricing or delivery dates if needed.
7. In **Step 7 (Review & Save)**, click **Save & Preview Proposal**.

### Method B: From the 7-Step Proposal Wizard
1. In the sidebar, click **+ New Proposal** (or **Create New Proposal** on the Dashboard).
2. Follow the guided steps:
   - **Step 1 · Client:** Select an existing client or enter new contact information.
   - **Step 2 · Project Details:** Enter project title, executive overview, start date, and estimated duration.
   - **Step 3 · Services & Scope:** Add your billable deliverables, quantity, units (hours, project, assets), and rates.
   - **Step 4 · Pricing & Taxes:** Apply an optional discount (percentage or fixed amount) and verify tax rates.
   - **Step 5 · Payment Terms:** Choose your milestone breakdown (e.g., 50% deposit / 50% completion, 30/70, or custom milestones).
   - **Step 6 · Scope Boundaries:** Protect yourself from scope creep! Check off what is included, what is excluded, revision rounds (e.g., 2 rounds), and client asset deadlines.
   - **Step 7 · Final Review:** Preview the financial breakdown and click **Save & Open Proposal**.

---

## Step 5: Export to PDF & Share with Your Client

When your proposal document opens in the viewer:

1. **Review the Executive Layout:** Verify your studio header, itemized pricing table, deliverables checklist, payment milestone breakdown, and revision policy.
2. **Export / Print PDF:**
   - Click the dark **Export / Print PDF** button in the top action toolbar.
   - In your browser's print dialog:
     - Set **Destination** to **Save as PDF**.
     - Set **Layout** to **Portrait**.
     - Under **More settings**, ensure **Background graphics** is **checked** (this preserves all styled background cards and borders).
     - Set **Margins** to **Default** or **None**.
   - Click **Save** to generate your client-ready PDF file.
3. **Share Link:**
   - Click **Share Link** to copy the digital proposal view URL to your clipboard.
4. **Mark as Sent:**
   - Click **Mark as Sent** to update the proposal's status on your dashboard pipeline.

---

## Step 6: Backup Your Data

Because QuotePilot stores your data in your browser's local memory for 100% privacy:
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Data Management & Backup**.
3. Click **Export JSON Backup**.
4. Store this `.json` file in your Google Drive, Dropbox, or backup folder.
5. If you ever switch browsers or buy a new laptop, simply click **Import JSON** in Settings to instantly restore everything!

**Congratulations! Your professional proposal studio is fully operational.**
